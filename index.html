MailVoice

Your new emails, read to you as short voice briefs on Telegram, and answered by voice.

What it does
  1. When a new email arrives in your inbox, MailVoice writes a short summary and turns it into a voice message.
  2. You get the voice brief and the written summary in Telegram. You can ask for the whole email to be read aloud.
  3. To answer, you reply with a voice message. MailVoice transcribes it and shows you the text.
  4. The email is sent only after you tap Send. Nothing is ever sent without your confirmation.

Languages

Romanian and English.

Google account access

MailVoice connects to your Gmail account only after you sign in with Google and grant access. It uses that access to read new messages in your inbox, mark them as read at your request, and send the replies you confirm. You can remove its access at any time from your Google Account.

See the Privacy Policy for exactly what data is used and how.

Status

MailVoice is a personal, self-hosted project in early testing. It is not offered to the public.
