Privacy Policy

MailVoice · Last updated: 30 September 2026

MailVoice is a personal, self-hosted assistant that reads new emails aloud as voice briefs on Telegram and sends replies dictated by the account owner. This policy explains what data it accesses, how that data is used, and how it is protected.

1. Data accessed

With your permission, granted through Google sign-in (scope https://mail.google.com/), MailVoice accesses:

  • New messages arriving in your Gmail inbox: sender, recipients, subject, date, message identifiers and the text of the message body. Attachments are ignored: they are not processed, stored or sent anywhere.
  • The ability to mark a message as read, only when you ask for it.
  • The ability to send email from your account, only for replies you have reviewed and confirmed.

It also receives the voice messages and text you send to the MailVoice Telegram bot, and your Telegram user ID.

2. How the data is used

  • To write a short summary of each new email and convert it into a voice message.
  • To read the full email aloud when you request it.
  • To transcribe your voice replies, tidy the punctuation, show you the draft, and send it as a reply in the same email thread after you confirm.

The data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to build profiles. It is not used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

3. Services that process the data

To provide these features, the minimum necessary content is sent to:

  • OpenRouter (openrouter.ai), which routes requests to AI model providers (such as Google and OpenAI) for summarising the email, generating speech, and transcribing voice replies. Only what is needed for the task is sent: the sender's name and address, the subject and the text of the email being summarised, or the voice message being transcribed.
  • Telegram, to deliver the voice briefs and summaries to you and receive your replies.
  • Google (Gmail), to receive and send email.

No other third party receives your data.

4. Storage and retention

  • MailVoice runs on a device controlled by the account owner, not on a shared cloud service.
  • Email text is kept only as long as needed to read it aloud and to reply, and is automatically deleted after 7 days. Sender, subject and summary are kept so replies can be matched to the right thread.
  • The text of replies you dictate and send is kept as a record of what was sent from your account.
  • Generated audio is created in memory, sent to Telegram, and never written to disk.
  • Google access tokens and email passwords are stored encrypted.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

MailVoice's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Your control

  • You can revoke MailVoice's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. MailVoice then stops reading your email immediately.
  • You can pause delivery at any time with the /pause command in Telegram.
  • You can ask for all stored data to be deleted by writing to the contact address below.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
